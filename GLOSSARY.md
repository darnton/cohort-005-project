# Course Platform

An online course platform where learners buy courses, watch lessons, and can ask for their money back.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund**:
A learner's request to get back the money for one Purchase, in full. A Refund is _Requested_, then a person reviews it and it becomes _Approved_ or _Declined_.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund that is still _Requested_ and waiting for someone to review it.
_Avoid_: Pending Chargeback, open reversal

**Approved Refund**:
A Refund where the learner gets back what they paid for the Purchase and loses access to the course.

**Declined Refund**:
A Refund that was turned down. No money is returned and the learner keeps access.

**Refund Amount**:
What the learner actually paid for the Purchase being refunded.

**Refund Window**:
The period after a Purchase during which the learner may request a Refund.
_Avoid_: Cancel window

**Refundable**:
A Purchase that is still inside its Refund Window, has no Pending Refund, and passes the watch, completion and team-seat rules.

**Chargeback**:
A dispute the cardholder's bank starts to force the money back. It is not something a learner starts on the platform, and it is never another word for a Refund.
